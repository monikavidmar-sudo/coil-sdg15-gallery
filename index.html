Why did we choose SDG 15?

About SDG 15

Sustainable Development Goal 15 is called Life on Land. Its purpose is to protect nature, forests, plants and animals, and preserve their habitats. The goal promotes the sustainable use of natural resources and aims to prevent the extinction of animal and plant species. It is important to preserve nature for future generations as well.

Challenges related to SDG 15

The main challenges related to Sustainable Development Goal 15 include deforestation, environmental pollution, habitat destruction, and the extinction of animal and plant species. Climate change, wildfires, and the overexploitation of natural resources are also major problems. These issues reduce biodiversity and threaten the balance of nature.
