01 / UNDERSTAND

About SDG 15

The United Nations adopted 17 Sustainable Development Goals in 2015 to guide action towards a more sustainable world by 2030. Goal 15 focuses on life on land: protecting and restoring ecosystems, sustainably managing forests, combating desertification and stopping biodiversity loss.

01

Why forests matter

Forests store carbon, protect soils, regulate water and provide habitat for countless species. Their health also supports communities and livelihoods.

02

What biodiversity means

Biodiversity includes the variety of species, genes and ecosystems. A diverse natural world helps ecosystems remain resilient.

03

The biggest threats

Deforestation, land degradation, pollution, invasive species and climate change put terrestrial ecosystems under pressure.

04

The goal for 2030

SDG 15 calls for conservation, restoration, sustainable land use and stronger action against habitat and species loss.

02 / LOCAL ECOSYSTEMS

SDG 15 in Slovenia

More than half of Slovenia is forested. From the Julian Alps to the Dinaric forests, the country is home to remarkable wildlife and protected landscapes. Conservation must balance healthy habitats with human activity.

Forests and biodiversity

Slovenian forests are home to brown bears, wolves, Eurasian lynx, deer and many bird species. Connected habitats help wildlife survive.

Protection and challenges

Triglav National Park and Natura 2000 sites help safeguard habitats. Fragmentation, invasive species, storms, drought and pests remain concerns.

03 / GLOBAL PERSPECTIVE

SDG 15 Around the World

Environmental challenges vary across countries. Explore five real forest regions on the interactive map to learn how deforestation, habitat fragmentation and biodiversity loss affect ecosystems.

Deforestation

Clearing forests for agriculture and other land uses removes habitat and disrupts ecosystems.

Habitat fragmentation

Roads and land conversion can divide wildlife populations and interrupt movement.

Degraded land

Soil damage, unsustainable land use and pollution can make ecosystems harder to restore.

04 / TAKE ACTION

Solutions and Actions

Conservation requires long-term protection, careful land management and ecological restoration. Explore practical approaches — then test your knowledge with a final SDG 15 quiz.

Restore native vegetation

Support natural regeneration and plant appropriate native species where restoration is needed.

Reconnect habitats

Protect wildlife corridors so animals can move between forest areas.

Protect threatened species

Safeguard important habitats, reduce human pressures and support conservation monitoring.

Choose sustainable practices

Reduce waste, support responsible products and land use, and participate in local conservation.
