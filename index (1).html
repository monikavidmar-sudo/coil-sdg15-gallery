PAGE 1 · UNDERSTAND

What is SDG 15?

Sustainable Development Goal 15, also known as Life on Land, is one of the 17 goals adopted by the United Nations in 2015. It focuses on protecting and restoring terrestrial ecosystems, managing forests sustainably, combating desertification, reversing land degradation and stopping biodiversity loss.

🌳 Why nature matters

Forests help regulate the climate, store carbon and protect water and soil. Healthy soils support agriculture, while plants and animals form interconnected ecosystems. Biodiversity makes these ecosystems more resilient to change.

Human wellbeing depends on functioning natural systems, from clean water and fertile land to pollination and natural resources.

💗 Why we chose this goal

We chose SDG 15 because nature is a part of everyday life and deserves protection. We want to show how human activities can affect forests, plants and animals—and how everyone can make thoughtful choices to help.

Our project combines information, photographs, videos and games to make learning about nature more engaging.

Challenges facing life on land

🌲 Deforestation

Clearing forests for agriculture, infrastructure and other uses can destroy habitats and reduce carbon storage.

🦋 Biodiversity loss

Habitat destruction, pollution, invasive species, overexploitation and climate change put species at risk.

🌱 Land degradation

Soil erosion, unsustainable land use and drought can make land less productive and damage ecosystems.

🗑️ Pollution

Waste and harmful substances can contaminate soil and water and affect wildlife.

🔥 Climate change

Rising temperatures and changing rainfall patterns put additional pressure on habitats and species.

🐾 Habitat fragmentation

Roads and development can divide natural habitats, making it harder for wildlife to move and survive.

PAGE 2 · TAKE ACTION

How can we solve these problems?

Protecting life on land requires cooperation between governments, communities, businesses and individuals. No single action can solve every problem, but practical changes can make a real difference.

🌳 Protect and restore forests

Protect existing forests, prevent illegal logging, restore degraded areas and plant suitable native trees where restoration is needed. Long-term care is as important as planting.

🐝 Support biodiversity

Protect natural habitats, grow native plants, avoid disturbing wildlife and support protected areas. Different species need different kinds of habitats to thrive.

♻️ Reduce waste

Use reusable items, repair products, recycle correctly and dispose of waste responsibly. Preventing pollution helps protect land and waterways.

🌾 Care for soil and land

Support sustainable farming, prevent erosion and use water responsibly. Healthy soil is vital for plants, animals and food production.

📚 Learn and share

Learn about local ecosystems and share trustworthy information. Community clean-ups and school projects can help people work together.

🏡 Make everyday choices

Buy only what you need, choose responsibly sourced products when possible and respect marked paths in nature. Small habits add up when many people take part.

🍄 Forest networks

Fungi help break down organic matter, and many form partnerships with tree roots that help plants obtain nutrients.

🐝 Pollinators

Bees, butterflies, birds and other animals help many flowering plants reproduce by moving pollen.

🌿 Soil is alive

Healthy soil contains many organisms, including bacteria, fungi and invertebrates, which help recycle nutrients.
